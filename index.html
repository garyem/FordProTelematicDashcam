Driver guide
Ford Pro Telematics dashcam
For all driver technicians
Your vehicle will soon get a new dashcam system. Here's what it does.
This guide explains clearly and simply what the Ford Pro Telematics dashcam captures, how it works, and what it means for you day to day.
What it records
The road ahead
HD forward-facing camera

A forward-facing 1080p HD camera records the road at all times. The lens covers around 96° of view.

  • Traffic, junctions and pedestrians
  • Collisions and near misses
  • Lane movement and other vehicles
  • Harsh braking incidents
Inside the cab
Inward-facing wide-angle camera

The system also includes an inward-facing camera with around 144° cabin coverage. This is the part many drivers do not expect.

  • Driver posture and attention
  • Seatbelt use
  • Phone usage detection (AI)
  • Eating, drinking or smoking
  • Signs of fatigue or distraction
The inward camera has infrared — so it records in low light and at night too.
How it monitors driving
Driving behaviour
Telematics data, not just video

The system connects to your vehicle's own data. That means it does not rely on video alone.

Harsh brakingSpeeding Sharp corneringHarsh acceleration Excessive idlingLane movement Collision detectionAirbag trigger

If any of these happen, the system may automatically save video from just before and after the event.

Location and route tracking
GPS built in

The telematics platform includes GPS tracking. Fleet managers can see:

  • Vehicle location at any time
  • Route history and journey playback
  • Stop durations and time on site
  • Arrival and departure times
  • Where speeding events happened
AI driver analysis
Automated, real-time

The system uses AI to analyse driver behaviour automatically. You do not need to do anything to trigger it — it works in the background.

  • Detects phone use, fatigue, distraction
  • Can generate driver safety scores
  • May give in-cab audio alerts in real time
  • Flags events for manager review

Managers do not watch live footage all day. The AI flags events automatically, and managers can then review those specific clips.

Your event button
The manual event button
Your vehicle has a button you can press yourself to mark and protect footage. Use it after an incident, near miss, or to record aggressive driving by others. It helps protect you.
What it does and doesn't do
It does
  • Record road & cab video
  • Track location & route
  • Detect driving events
  • Flag incidents to managers
  • Record in the dark
It doesn't
  • Read your phone contents
  • Record audio 24/7
  • Livestream every moment
  • Listen like a microphone
Reasons why this system is used
This protects you too
If someone makes a false claim against you, this footage is your evidence. It protects you from unfair blame, disputes with other drivers, and insurance issues.
Good to know
Footage can also be used in disciplinary investigations. Drive as you would if you knew someone was watching — because in some cases, they can be.
Data and storage
How footage is kept
  • Up to 128GB stored locally on the device
  • Normal footage overwrites after a period
  • Flagged events are kept for longer
  • Important clips may upload to secure cloud
